Name

In Vietnamese name order, the family name comes first, followed by the middle name and given name. My Vietnamese name is Do Quoc Trong: Do is my family name, Quoc is my middle name, and Trong is my given name.

In English, "Trong" is direct to pronounce; "Do" sounds close to the "o" in "bro"; and "Quoc" sounds close to "quote".
