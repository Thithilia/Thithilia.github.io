Starting a research notebook online

I want this blog to be a place where I can write in a slower and more careful way about physics, learning, and research. Some posts may be polished essays; others may simply be notes that preserve a question, calculation, or reading path.

The goal is not to make every thought final. The goal is to keep a record of how ideas develop, especially when studying theoretical physics, general relativity, particle physics, and physics education.

Future posts can replace this sample article with real notes, research summaries, or short reflections from courses and papers.
