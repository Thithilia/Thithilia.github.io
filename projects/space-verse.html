Community project

Space-Verse Website

Space-Verse is a community-led educational platform created to make space science, astronomy, Earth observation, and space technology more accessible to Vietnamese learners.

Visit the live website View the GitHub repository

About the project

I co-founded Space-Verse together with fellow alumni of the Department of Space and Earth Sciences, formerly known as the Department of Space and Applications, at the University of Science and Technology of Hanoi (USTH). The project grew from our shared interest in space science and our wish to make useful academic information easier for Vietnamese high school students, university students, and early-career researchers to find and understand.

What Space-Verse offers

  • Research areas. Introductions to astrophysics and cosmology, satellite technology, remote sensing and Earth sciences, and space physics.
  • Learning resources. A structured catalogue covering foundational mathematics and physics, scientific programming, academic skills, and more advanced space-science subjects.
  • Study opportunities. Guidance for graduate programmes, internships, and scholarships, with links back to official sources for application details.
  • Curated news. Short Vietnamese summaries of space-related developments that preserve the original source, publication date, topic, and image credit.

My contribution

I contribute to the development of the website, its information architecture, and the ongoing organization and expansion of its educational content. Space-Verse remains a collaborative project, with its direction shaped by the experience and interests of the wider team.

Development

The public site is built as a responsive multilingual website and deployed through GitHub Pages. Its repository includes automated browser tests for core pages and responsive layouts. It also contains the foundation for a source-aware news workflow using Supabase Edge Functions and scheduled GitHub Actions, while keeping public content traceable to its original sources.
